🏫 MY TEACHER SAID
Is it true that Manusmriti says bad things about women?
💬
Someone says: "Doesn't Manusmriti treat women as inferior?"
🟠 Quick Answer · 20 sec
Yes, controversial verses exist. But we don't have the original Manusmriti, surviving manuscripts differ, and the same text tells families to honor women — saying a family prospers when its women are respected and happy.

What I Can Say
Yes, those verses exist in versions we have today. But we don't have Manu's original, the manuscripts differ, and the same Manusmriti says families prosper where women are honored. So it's more complicated than one quotation.
📖 Understand the full story
⌄
① Yes, those verses exist
Some versions of the Manusmriti we have today do contain verses about women that we'd consider unfair by today's standards. That's real — don't pretend otherwise.
② But we don't have the original
The text passed through many centuries of handwritten copies. More than fifty surviving manuscripts have been studied by scholars, and they don't all say exactly the same thing.
③ Here's what else Manusmriti says
The same text contains one of its most famous lines — and it says the opposite.
Where women are honored, there the Devatās rejoice; where women are not honored, all sacred actions become fruitless.
Manusmriti 3.56
