What is Sanātana FAQ?

This is a resource for Hindu American students in grades 6–8 who get questions about Sanātana Dharma from friends, classmates, teachers, or things they see online.

Each answer has three levels:

🟠Quick Answer — understand it in 20 seconds
💬What I can say — words you can actually use
📖Full Story — learn enough to really understand it

Parents, teachers, and Bal Vihar educators can open the sources for deeper reading at the bottom of each answer.

The questions shown by default are popular ones to get you started — not the only questions here. Search any word to see everything.
