WHY A4H ENDORSES

Jenny Rae
Le Roux

for U.S. House — California 47th District

A4H is proud to endorse Jenny Rae Le Roux for U.S. House in California's 47th Congressional District. In her direct conversation with A4H leadership and members, Jenny demonstrated a willingness to listen, engage seriously with the concerns of Hindu Americans, and work with our community on issues where our values and priorities align.

Most importantly, she committed to working with A4H on the areas discussed that align with our values and to joining the Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist and Jain (HSBJ) Caucus. A4H believes political engagement should lead to meaningful relationships and accountability, and Jenny's willingness to make these commitments was an important factor in our endorsement.

🤝

Direct Engagement & Commitment to A4H Priorities

Jenny participated directly in an A4H call and heard from our leadership and community about issues important to Hindu Americans. She expressed her willingness to work on the areas discussed that align with A4H values and priorities. For A4H, this kind of direct engagement matters. Our endorsements are intended to build relationships with elected leaders who will listen to our community and remain engaged after Election Day.

🕉️

Commitment to Join the HSBJ Caucus

Jenny committed to joining the Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist and Jain Caucus. A4H considers participation in the HSBJ Caucus an important way for elected officials to better understand and address issues affecting Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist and Jain Americans and to ensure these communities have an organized voice in government.

⚖️

Equal Voice & Fair Treatment for Hindu Americans

A4H believes Hindu Americans should receive the same dignity, access, and consideration afforded to every other American community. Jenny's conversation with A4H demonstrated a willingness to listen to concerns affecting Hindu Americans and to work constructively with our community rather than allowing stereotypes or ideological labels to define it.

💼

Economic Opportunity & Entrepreneurship

As an entrepreneur and business leader, Jenny brings firsthand experience with building organizations, creating opportunity, and navigating the challenges facing businesses and working families. A4H values policies that encourage entrepreneurship, innovation, economic mobility, and an environment in which individuals can advance through education, hard work, initiative, and merit.

🎓

Education, Merit & Upward Mobility

Education, achievement, and upward mobility are deeply important to many Hindu American and Indian American families. A4H appreciates leaders who recognize the importance of educational excellence, merit, opportunity, and preparing the next generation to compete and succeed.

🛡️

Public Safety & Strong Communities

A4H supports practical policies that keep families and neighborhoods safe while strengthening confidence in public institutions. Jenny's solutions-oriented approach and emphasis on effective government align with A4H's preference for measurable results over ideological politics.

🌟

Respectful Engagement with California's Diverse Communities

California is home to one of the largest and most vibrant Hindu American and Indian American populations in the country. Jenny's willingness to speak directly with A4H, hear our concerns, and establish an ongoing relationship demonstrates the type of engagement A4H expects from candidates seeking the community's support.

🏛️

An Endorsement Built on Engagement — and Accountability

A4H endorsements are not simply election-season statements. We want enduring relationships with leaders who understand our community, engage with us, and follow through on commitments. Jenny Rae Le Roux's direct engagement with A4H, her commitment to work with us on areas of shared values, and her commitment to join the HSBJ Caucus provide a strong foundation for that relationship. A4H looks forward to working with her and to maintaining that engagement and accountability after the election.
